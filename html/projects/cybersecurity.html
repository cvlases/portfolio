Working for the Cyber Operations Bureau

Interning at the Montana State Information Technology Services Division, I worked alongside a team balancing innovation with security while serving the Montana public.

// the work

At the Cyber Operations Bureau, I helped automate cybersecurity response playbooks, speeding up the state’s response to threat intelligence and supporting real operational workflows.

// policy

I also created a comprehensive AI policy document for the Risk Management Bureau, which gave me a close-up view of the risks and benefits of AI in Montana state government.

// mentorship

Under the mentorship of Chris Santucci, I got to see firsthand what public-interest technology work looks like when it is grounded in responsibility, service, and trust.

// what stayed with me

I developed a stronger understanding of day-to-day cyber operations, contributed to projects that are actively being implemented, built meaningful relationships with the team, and left with a much clearer sense of how security and policy shape public systems.
